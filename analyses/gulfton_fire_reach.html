How to read this. Distances are straight-line from each fire to the nearest of 90 HFD stations; airport rescue stations are excluded. Drive distance is longer, especially for Station 28, which has the Southwest Freeway and the Westpark Tollway between it and Gulfton. Streets shown are Vision Zero high-injury corridors, used here as reference lines only; they are not a complete street map. Fire records come from the City of Houston structural-fires layer (2015 to June 2021), and station locations are current. Sources: HFD, COH GIS, and the houston-db warehouse.
